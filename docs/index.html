Tri-Swiss

Two colors, one accent, one genuine third

A genuine third color.

Lux Solari's house design language — Swiss-minimalist, tri-tone, more colorful. Two structural colors, one strong accent, one governed highlight; everything else is weight, space, and contrast. Two rules govern everything

01 — Tri-tone, more colorful

Ink and cream still lead. primary, Swiss Red, is the primary accent: action, destructive, focus, the real hover signal, an emphasized section divider, one selective card border, a Structural Block. highlight, Pastel Turquoise, stays non-semantic but recurs as pure decoration and has a smaller Structural Block of its own. Two named exceptions aside, the tri-part stripe (spatial) and the dual-accent hover swap (sequential), neither color carries state meaning nor touches the other.

02 — Swiss-minimalist

Borders are 1px solid border, full ink in light and full cream in dark. No shadows: elevation is the one background step from background to card. Whitespace is generous. Labels are uppercase mono with 0.2em tracking. Corners are square; radius exists but is rarely used, and rounded-full is for 6px dots only.

“Red and Turquoise are a deliberate, governed layer on top — never a substitute for discipline.”

Using the system

  1. 01Copy skills/tri-swiss/assets/theme.css into your global stylesheet. It defines every token for light and dark, both font flavors and the Jost accent, wired to Tailwind 4 via @theme inline; on other stacks the :root / .dark / .geist / .jost blocks work as plain custom properties.
  2. 02Pick a font flavor, Space (default) or Geist, and load only that flavor's two families plus Zilla Slab from Google Fonts. Add Jost 700 only if the heading accent is on.
  3. 03Build with the semantic token, never a raw hex. Reach for bg-highlight / text-highlight decoratively only: icon fills, underlines, washes, a chart's genuine third series, a callout or closing band paired with text-highlight-foreground. Never for a button, tag, pip or link, and never to signal state on its own.

Dark mode is the .dark class on <html>, persisted in localStorage under theme. The Geist flavor is .geist; the Jost heading accent is .jost. All three compose independently — try the toggles in the sidebar.

House Mark

How Tri-Swiss and its sibling Lux Swiss share governance and differ in palette.

Do not

  • No third semantic color. highlight is not success green or info blue in disguise.
  • No accent-colored hover signal other than Red, except the two named dual-accent patterns.
  • No shadows. Depth is border presence and one background step.
  • No rounded-full on containers or pills. Dots only.
  • No raw hex in markup. Always the semantic token.
  • No chart libraries except restyled Observable Plot.
  • No icons but restyled geist-icons. No emoji in UI text.
  • No Turquoise on buttons, tags, status pips or links as their rest color.
  • No Red and Turquoise simultaneously on the same element. The stripe and the dual-accent hover are the two exceptions.
  • No accent-colored list markers or table borders.
  • No full-color images outside the photography-content exception.
  • Ink and cream still dominate. A surface where Red or Turquoise out-covers them has gone too far.
